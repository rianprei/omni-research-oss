# Trend intelligence

Use `social_search` (Hacker News, Stack Overflow, public Reddit), `news_search` and `web_search`. Set a precise research window and check dates in the **original** item; RSS/feed timestamps can differ from event timestamps. Public social endpoints may be blocked or sampled, so report missing platforms explicitly. X, TikTok, YouTube and other gated services are **not** bundled as unrestricted free search providers.

Separate engagement (votes, comments, ranking) from prevalence, factual reliability and representativeness. Avoid extrapolating from a self-selected sample. Search conflicting viewpoints and independent confirmation. Output: what changed, when, evidence sources, community reaction, counterevidence and data/coverage limits.
